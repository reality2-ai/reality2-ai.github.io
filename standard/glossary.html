Glossary

Every term the standard defines, and the clause that defines it. 81 entries drawn from the terms-and-definitions clause of each part.

This page is derived, and it is not the standard. Every entry is reproduced from a clause, and the clause is the normative text — follow the link before relying on a definition.

It overlaps the Terminology part deliberately. Terminology defines the 10 terms the whole standard shares; each other part defines the terms it needs for itself. All of both are here, which is the point of a glossary — but Terminology is a part of the standard and this is an index of one clause from every part.

1 headword carries a different meaning in two parts and is marked two meanings — read the note. Both clauses carry a note pointing at the other and prescribing the qualified form, so neither can be read without the reader being told. Where a part only lists a term and points at the definitive entry, that pointer is not reproduced here — the definition it points to is.
