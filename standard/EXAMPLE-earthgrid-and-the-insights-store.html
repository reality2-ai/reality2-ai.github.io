Reality2 Standard — worked example: a community earthgrid and the insights store

Status: Informative — worked example Date: 2026-08-03

⚠ INFORMATIVE. The fourth worked example, and the first with long-lived entanglements and more than two parties. Payment was minutes, the room was hours, the stadium was an evening; this one runs for years, and its subject is the thing the other three only brushed: trust as a graded, earned, local, decaying quantity — and what happens to a party that breaches it. This is Trust, not Authority, and it is the Thurisaz technique's home ground: value transactions. Open edges are flagged in place.

Corpus input (informative): the working corpus's marketplace and reputation drafts (TH-MARKET, TH-REP, TH-WEAVE, MK-ACCESS) supply much of this machinery in earlier form; they are input to the companion documents, not requirements of this standard.

Cast

  • Community trust group C — an earthgrid: river-level sensors, rain gauges, a camera at the sandbar, all in one trust group owned by the community. The people of the community are its members; they manage the sensors with their own hands and their own management surfaces.
  • The insights store, trust group S — a facility communities and organisations both entangle with. It runs a catalogue, relays requests, settles payment. It never holds raw data and it never grants access to anyone else's.
  • Organisation trust group O — an insurance company, building a flood-exposure report: towns on floodplains with a sandbar across the tidal river.
  • Many more communities — C is one of hundreds entangled with S, each sovereign, each different.

The topology: a hub of entanglements, and what the hub is not

  C1 ─────┐
  C2 ─────┤  long-term        long-term  ┌───── O1 (insurer)
  C3 ─────┼── entanglements ── S ────────┤
  ...     │      (years)        (years)  └───── O2
  Cn ─────┘
  • C ↔︎ S: a long-term entanglement whose conditions are the community's terms of trade — which insight classes may be offered, to what kinds of buyer, under what licence, at what price floor, with payment terms. Scoped, levelled, conditional, severable (L5 10.2) — exactly the same contract shape as the payment example's ninety-second entanglement, with the lifetime dial turned to years.
  • O ↔︎ S: the same shape from the buyer's side — what O may request, what it may do with what it buys, how it pays.
  • C ↔︎ O: nothing. The insurer is never entangled with any community. The store is a hub of pairwise agreements, not a root of authority — it cannot see C's raw data, cannot speak for C, and cannot grant O anything that is C's to give.

Distance changes nothing: C's bridge hive reaches S over an internet bearer, and an entanglement is indifferent to which bearers carry it — the same clause 10 contract that ran over one room's radio runs over an ocean.

The data sovereignty rule, stated before any request arrives

Raw observations never leave the community's trust group. What crosses the boundary to S — and only ever under the entanglement's terms — is a derived insight: a conclusion computed inside C, carrying its confidence, its time bounds, and its provenance. The river's minute-by-minute levels are the community's; "flood risk at the north bridge: moderate, confidence 0.6, next six hours" is a product. The distinction is the business model and the privacy model, and it is enforced where everything in this standard is enforced: at C's own delivery gate, by C's own policy, on C's own hives.

The request: one question, many sovereign answers

The insurer asks S for floodplain-town exposure. What happens next is the heart of this example:

  1. S consults its catalogue and relays a derived request to the communities whose offerings could bear on it — coastal, tidal-river, sandbar-flagged earthgrids. The request carries what O is, what it wants, and under which licence class.
  2. Each community judges for itself. There is no central grant. C's policy sentant — configured by the community, escalating to a person for anything unusual — weighs the request against the terms of its own entanglement with S: is this buyer class admitted? this insight class offered? this licence acceptable? this price right? And one more input, which is where Thurisaz enters: how much does C currently believe about O's good conduct?
  3. Some communities answer — an insight event, encrypted under the C ↔︎ S entanglement key, priced, provenance attached. Some decline by silence. A community that does not wish to serve an insurer does not file a refusal or plead an exception; its gate simply does not open. Not blocked; unheeded — the same posture the protester met at the council screen, now doing commercial work.
  4. S assembles what was given — never what wasn't — settles payment along both entanglements, and the communities that contributed are paid. Value moves as the payment example moves it; what the tokens are is, as ever, the parties' agreement.

The report the insurer receives is not "the data." It is the union of what several dozen sovereign groups each independently chose to sell that morning, at the confidence each was willing to stand behind.

The breach, and why nobody revokes anything

A year later the insurer breaches its terms — say it redistributes licensed insights to a subsidiary the licence never covered. Now watch what does not happen: no licence is revoked, no account is suspended, no authority hands down a ban. There is no authority to do it. What happens instead is graded, local, and everywhere at once:

  • Evidence arrives and is weighed, not obeyed. A breach claim is a claim like any other — it enters each party's Thurisaz weave with a weight set by who asserts it and what supports it. S, holding the transaction records, forms its belief first.
  • S demotes the O ↔︎ S entanglement — the breach is a condition failure, and L5 10.3 offers exactly two responses: sever, or move down the ladder. S moves O down: still entangled, at a level that now admits only view-only licences, or only aggregate insights, or nothing without prepayment.
  • Each community updates its own belief. S's observation reaches the communities as an attested claim under their entanglements — believed to the degree their conditions say S is believable, corroborated or not by their own transaction history with O's requests. Each C adjusts its own number. No two communities need agree, and none can be made to.
  • The next request finds a different world. O asks again; the request goes out; and this time fewer gates open. Some communities decline outright, some quote higher, some offer coarser insights at lower confidence, a few — unpersuaded by the evidence, or indifferent — trade as before, as is their right. The consequence is the aggregate of independent judgements, which is precisely why it cannot be appealed, bribed, or seized: there is no single mind to capture.

And because belief is Thurisaz-shaped, the standing decays toward neutral rather than freezing at the verdict — conduct re-earns what conduct spent. A party that breaches is not branded forever; it is disbelieved for a while, in proportion, and the way back is the same way everyone got in: transactions that survive scrutiny. The corpus's reputation draft gives group-level standing a half-life of days-to-weeks; the number is a dial, but the shape — evidence in, decay toward neutral, redemption by conduct — is the design.

Trust, not Authority — the distinction this example exists to draw

Authority Trust
Lives inside a group (L6): update authority, membership by hands between groups: entanglements, reputation, standing
Form binary — held or not held, enumerable (L6 4.1) graded — a belief between 0 and 1, never done moving
Granted by the group, by ceremony nobody — earned transaction by transaction
Removed by revocation — one act, total effect decay and demotion — many local judgements, proportional effect
Wrong answer looks like a forbidden act performed a counterparty nobody will deal with

The community's members hold authority over their own sensors — that is L6, inside the boundary, binary and ceremonial. Everything between C, S and O runs on trust — graded, local, decaying, priced. The stack keeps them apart because they fail differently: captured authority is a coup; poisoned trust is a market correction.

This is also why the earlier examples' machinery needed no extension here. The entanglement contract (payment), silence toward the unentitled (the room), evidence believed per conditions (the stadium's attestation) — the same dials, with lifetime turned to years and belief doing the work that a central authority does in every system this standard declines to be.
