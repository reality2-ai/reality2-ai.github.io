The Reality2 Standard

A decentralised mesh protocol for trust groups. These are the normative parts — the text an implementation is measured against.

Status. This standard is under active development. Clauses marked PROVISIONAL are drafted and not yet ratified. Provenance records, the open-questions register and the requirements registers are internal and are not published here — where the text cites them, it is naming an internal record rather than a link you can follow.

Read in this order
