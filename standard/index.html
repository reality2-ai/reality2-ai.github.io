The Reality2 Standard

Unversioned draft

A decentralised mesh protocol for trust groups. These are the normative parts — the text an implementation is measured against.

Status. This standard is under active development. Clauses marked PROVISIONAL are drafted and not yet ratified. Provenance records, the open-questions register and the requirements registers are internal and are not published here — where the text cites them, it is naming an internal record rather than a link you can follow.

Read in this order

The overview says what the parts are. Terminology defines the words every other part uses; nothing below it will read correctly without it. The layers then build upward, and FORMATS is the byte-level companion the wire parts refer to.

OverviewWhat Reality2 is, what the parts are, and the order to read them in.TerminologyDefined terms. Every other part depends on these.L0 — Hardware and runtimeWhat a platform provides to the layers above: identity, storage, entropy, power.L1 — TransportThe contract every bearer satisfies, whatever medium it uses.L2 — Capability and discoveryBeacons, what a hive announces, and what a scanner may infer.L3 — RoutingRelaying without knowing anybody. The mesh below the trust boundary.L4 — WireThe frame: header, two tiers, addressing, and the end-to-end integrity envelope.L5 — Trust and identityThe trust group: identity, keys, membership, lifecycle, and the delivery gate.L5A — EntanglementEntanglement between two trust groups: the authority to bind one, and the grades of authentication.L5B — EnrolmentJoining a trust group, and the ceremony that admits a member.L5C — Management and walletThe management wallet: what it may hold, and custody separated from membership.L6 — ManagementWho may update, configure and inspect a hive — and what evidence each operation requires.L7 — ApplicationThe sentant: its defining properties, its definition, its lifecycle, and its event model.FORMATSThe byte-level formats the layer parts reference but do not state.
